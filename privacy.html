This website collects personal information only when you choose to submit it via the contact form.

What data is collected

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry. It is not used for marketing, profiling, or added to any mailing list.

How your data is processed

Form submissions are handled by Formspree, a third-party form processing service. Data is transmitted securely and stored only as needed to respond to your message.

Legal basis

The legal basis for processing this data is your explicit consent, given when you submit the contact form.

Data retention

Messages are retained only for as long as necessary to respond and for reasonable follow-up, after which they may be deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, you can contact us using the same contact form on this site.
